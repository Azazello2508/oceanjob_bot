V.Ships Ukraine

Crewing company

Country:

Ukraine

City: Odessa
Contact: Please login!
Site: http://vships.com.ua
License: AB №547298

Operating more than 25 years in Ukraine, V.Ships quickly became one of the most respected and stable companies in the country, specializing in crew management, training and recruitment for the world's leading shipowners, as well as “Trainer No.1” for cadets from main maritime educational institutions of Ukraine.

Every year more than 7,000 Ukrainian seafarers are employed on ships under foreign flags, which are under V.Ships Management - either full technical or crew. In addition, "V.Ships Ukraine" provides a shipboard practice, that guarantees further employment subject to good performance, for more than 400 Ukrainian cadets per year.

At the same time, all employment and training services for seafarers V.Ships provides free of charge, which is especially important today for the introduction of the concept of large-scale crewing, working at the level of international standards! Today V.Ships invites seafarers of all ranks for long-term cooperation in view of huge and extremely rapid expansion of the fleet!

We offer wide range of opportunities on dry bulk, container, car carrier and tanker fleets for officers and ratings!

We guarantee:
- Stable and non-commission employment;
- High and timely wages;
- System of bonuses and additional payments;
- Mixed crews;
- Fast career growth;
- Cadet Program;
- Medical insurance for seafarers and family members.

Hurry up to become a part of the V.Ships team!
